About

Undergraduate thesis

An Ensemble Deep Learning Framework for Two-Stage Diabetic Retinopathy Diagnosis and Severity Screening

East Delta University, February 2025. Published as paper [1].

  • Designed a two-stage pipeline: a DenseNet121 detector first decides whether diabetic retinopathy is present, then a severity model grades it.
  • Built a weighted-average ensemble of DenseNet121, Xception and EfficientNetB1 for four-grade severity classification.
  • Merged APTOS 2019, MESSIDOR-2 and IDRiD into a 5,922-image dataset and used augmentation to balance the grades.
  • Compared three fundus image preprocessing pipelines.
