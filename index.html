Undergraduate thesis

An Ensemble Deep Learning Framework for Two-Stage Diabetic Retinopathy Diagnosis and Severity Screening

East Delta University, July 2024 – January 2025. Published as first author at ICTIS 2025, paper [1].

  • Designed a two-stage diabetic retinopathy (DR) framework: a DenseNet121 detector first decides whether DR is present, then a weighted ensemble of DenseNet121, Xception and EfficientNetB1 grades it into four severity levels.
  • Merged APTOS 2019, MESSIDOR-2 and IDRiD into a 5,922-image dataset, and benchmarked three fundus image preprocessing pipelines.
